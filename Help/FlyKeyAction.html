Fly Key Action

The Fly Key Action picker determines what the Fly Key will do when pressed. Some options might not be available depending on your power picks and/or Speed on Demand settings.

  • Speed on Demand - toggle between Fly SoD Mode and your Default SoD Mode.
  • Power Toggle - toggle between your Primary and Defensive Fly Powers. If either is unavailable, this will toggle the other one on and off.
  • None - disabled.
