Jump Key Action

The Jump Key Action picker determines what the Jump Key will do when pressed. Some options might not be available depending on your power picks and/or Speed on Demand settings.

  • Speed on Demand - toggle between Jump SoD Mode and your Default SoD Mode:.
  • Power Toggle - toggle between your Primary and Defensive Jump Powers. If either is unavailable, this will toggle the other one on and off.
  • None - disabled.
