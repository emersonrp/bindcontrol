Super Jump During Jumps

With this checkbox checked, when a player is in SoD Mode, jumping will immediately activate the Jump Power for as long as the jump key (typically Space) is held down. When the jump key is released, the Jump Power will be deactivated.

Though this is a fun and quick way to get around, it might require some practice to avoid taking falling damage from releasing the key too early and switching out of Super Jump in mid-air.
