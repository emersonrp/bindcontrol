Toggle Super Jump While Jumping

With this checkbox checked, jumping will immediately activate the Jump Power for as long as the Up key (typically Space) is held down. When the Up key is released, the Jump Power will be deactivated.

This feature only works while Speed on Demand is active, and only in Sprint, Speed, or Non-SoD Modes.

Though this is a fun and quick way to get around, especially when paired with a Speed power, it might require some practice to avoid taking falling damage from releasing the Up key too early and switching off the Jump power in mid-air.
